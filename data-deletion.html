Data Deletion Instructions

Revolve Group, Inc. – Creator Partnership Discovery

You can stop the Creator Partnership Discovery app from accessing your data, and ask us to delete the data we hold, at any time. This page explains how. For how we handle data in general, see our Privacy Policy.

Step 1 – Remove the app from your Facebook account

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Open Business Integrations (also shown as Apps and Websites in some accounts).
  3. Select the app and choose Remove.

Removing the app revokes its access to your Facebook and Instagram data immediately. No further data is collected after that.

Step 2 – Ask us to delete the data we hold

Send an email to flavielle.blanco@revolve.com with the subject Data deletion request and include your Facebook or Instagram username, so we can find your records.

We will confirm your request and delete the associated data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and Facebook user ID, and the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional account identifiers linked to them, if you signed in to the app;
  • Any access tokens issued to the app for your account;
  • Creator data we stored about your Instagram professional account for reporting, such as profile information, media metrics (likes, comments, views, captions, timestamps, permalinks), aggregated audience insights, and branded-content media.

We may keep data that we are legally required to retain. Data we stored only in aggregated form cannot be linked back to you and is not affected.
