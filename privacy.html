Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who we are

Revolve Group, Inc. (“Revolve”, “we”, “us”), 12889 Moore Street, Cerritos, CA 90703, USA, operates the Creator Partnership Discovery application (the “App”) as an internal tool for its influencer-marketing team. The App is used to discover creators for brand partnerships and to measure the performance of public creator content. It is not offered to the general public.

2. Data we access through Meta Platforms

Facebook Login for Business. When a member of our team signs in, we access:

  • the user’s name and Facebook user ID;
  • the list of Facebook Pages the user manages, and the Instagram professional account linked to each of those Pages.

Instagram Graph API. We access:

  • Business Discovery: public profile information (such as username, name, biography, follower and media counts, profile picture) and public media metrics (likes, comments, views, captions, timestamps and permalinks) of Instagram professional accounts;
  • Instagram Creator Marketplace API: creator profiles (such as username, country, category, verification and onboarding status, and the gender and age bucket returned by Meta), aggregated audience insights (for example top audience countries, engaged accounts and reach), and branded-content media.

We do not access private messages, passwords, or personal (non-professional) Instagram accounts.

3. How we use it

We use this data only to find and evaluate creators for partnerships with Revolve brands and to report on campaign and content performance. We do not use it for advertising targeting, for profiling unrelated to partnerships, or for any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data obtained through Meta Platforms. We share it only with service providers that host or process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Storage and retention

Access tokens are kept only in the browser session in the demo and are never written to disk. Creator data used for reporting is retained for no longer than 24 months, after which it is deleted or aggregated.

6. Your choices and data deletion

You can remove the App at any time in Facebook under Settings → Business Integrations; this immediately revokes its access to your data. To ask us to delete data we hold about you or your Instagram professional account, follow the steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and access limited to authorized members of our team, to protect data against loss, theft, and unauthorized access, disclosure, or use.

8. Children

The App is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from people under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, and the “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
